# Technical Sheet — Weather Reference: Rain by Night

## SUBJECT OVERVIEW
Wet glass, spherical rain droplets, refracted city lights producing circular bokeh.  
Used when the protagonist is inside the GT86 during rain sequences.

Reference image: `/references/weather/rainbynight_reference.png`

---

## MATERIALS
- Wet glass  
- Raindrops (liquid water)  
- Reflective wet asphalt  

---

## COLOR PALETTE
| Color | Hex | Notes |
|-------|------|-------|
| Wet Red | `#E02020` | Traffic lights / neon reflection |
| Green Accent | `#2ECC40` | Secondary reflections |
| Yellow Light | `#FFDD00` | Street lamps |
| Black | `#000000` | Night base |

---

## GEOMETRY / FORM
- Hemispherical droplets (never flat)  
- Refraction of light into circular bokeh  
- Wet reflective surfaces in background  
- Soft transitions between light sources  

---

## AI GUIDELINES
- Depth of field must be shallow (focus on droplets)  
- Bokeh must remain circular, not polygonal  
- Avoid rendering raindrops as plastic beads  
- Reflections should remain blurred and diffused  

---

## NOTES FOR GENERATION
- Rain-by-night scenes emphasize interior mood and isolation.  
- Keep droplets crisp and foreground-focused.  
- Wet asphalt reflections should not overpower the frame.
