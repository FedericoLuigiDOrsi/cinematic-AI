# Technical Sheet — Weather Reference: Night (Clear or Foggy)

## SUBJECT OVERVIEW
Dominant dark sky illuminated by artificial light sources such as halogen or LED lamps.  
Used for the majority of the driving scenes and parking shots.

Reference images:  
- `/references/weather/night_reference_1.png`  
- `/references/weather/night_reference_2.png`

---

## MATERIALS
- Clear night sky or fog/mist layer  
- Halogen/LED artificial light  
- Moist ground reflections  

---

## COLOR PALETTE
| Color | Hex | Notes |
|-------|------|-------|
| Deep Black | `#000000` | Sky |
| Night Blue | `#0A1A4A` | Ambient tone |
| Halogen Yellow | `#F4D23C` | Light source |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Light bloom core |

---

## GEOMETRY / FORM
- Tall lamp posts  
- Strong light falloff  
- Atmospheric diffusion in presence of fog  
- Minimal visible background  

---

## AI GUIDELINES
- Maintain realistic light bloom (not excessive)  
- Fog should diffuse light softly, not blur everything  
- Avoid color-shifting lamps into blue/purple  
- Maintain contrast: bright light sources vs dark sky  

---

## NOTES FOR GENERATION
- Night defines the majority of the film's atmosphere.  
- Artificial lights should be **primary** visual anchors.  
- Surfaces (road, car windows) may reflect subtle highlights.
