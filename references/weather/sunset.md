# Technical Sheet — Weather Reference: Sunset

## SUBJECT OVERVIEW
Deep orange and red stratified cloud formations with high-contrast atmospheric gradients.  
Used for scenes around dusk and pre-night transition when the GT86 is visible in silhouette.

Reference images: `/references/weather/sunset_reference.png`
`/references/weather/sunset_reference2.png`

---

## MATERIALS
- Atmospheric haze  
- Stratus/Cirrus cloud vapor  
- Horizon silhouette contrast  

---

## COLOR PALETTE
| Color | Hex | Notes |
|-------|------|-------|
| Deep Orange | `#FF5A24` | Main sky gradient |
| Sunset Red | `#D43232` | Highlight clouds |
| Magenta Shadow | `#A0244E` | Transition shadow |
| Horizon Blue | `#1E4D6F` | Lower sky edge |

---

## GEOMETRY / FORM
- Layered cloud structures  
- Horizontal stratification  
- Gradient transitions from warm to cool  
- Defined horizon line  

---

## AI GUIDELINES
- Keep cloud layering intact  
- Avoid neon-like oversaturation  
- Maintain natural atmospheric gradient  
- Consistency across shots: warm top / cooler bottom  

---

## NOTES FOR GENERATION
- Sunset is used to introduce emotional warmth before night scenes.  
- Must remain **realistic, not stylized**.  
- Contrast should highlight silhouette elements (car, landscape).
