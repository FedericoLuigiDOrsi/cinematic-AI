## AI GUIDELINES & NOTES

### Consistency
- Maintain uniform **Blue Accent** shade on shoes.  
- Ensure **white Arc'teryx logo** always correctly formed.  
- Oakley visor should maintain single-piece smoked look.  

### Common Errors to Avoid
- Pants generated too slim or structured  
- Jacket changing reflectivity (must stay matte)  
- Misplaced Arc'teryx logo  
- Sunglasses turning into multi-piece frames  
- Shoes changing brand silhouette  

### Lighting / DOF
- Neutral softbox-style lighting for technical clarity  
- Medium DOF to preserve texture differences between suede, nylon, rubber  

### Notes for Generation
- Character has a relaxed technical aesthetic.  
- The combination of Arc'teryx jacket, Oakley sunglasses, and Salomon XT-4 shoes forms the **core identity**.  
- Maintain proportions: extremely baggy pants, straight-fit jacket, compact trail shoes.
