# Technical Sheet — Toyota GT86 Interior (Custom Blue Trim)

## SUBJECT OVERVIEW
Interior cabin of a Toyota GT86 featuring extensive aftermarket customization.  
The standard black leather and plastic interior is modified with vivid **blue suede/Alcantara** applied to:

- Steering wheel rim  
- Dashboard gauge hood  
- Center console knee pads  
- Shift boot + shift surround  
- Spherical shift knob  
- Handbrake handle  
- Vent rings  
- BRIDE logo headrest pillows  
- Seatbelt pads  

This reference sheet standardizes the color, materials, shapes, and interior geometry for maintaining consistency across AI-generated frames.

## MATERIALS

### Blue Suede / Alcantara  
- Texture: matte, napped, soft  
- High absorbance of light  
- Used on wheel cover, shift boot, pads, vent rings  

### Black Perforated Leather  
- Semi-gloss finish  
- Present on steering wheel grips and seat upholstery  

### Black Hard Plastic (Textured)  
- Dashboard, lower console, door trims  

### Blue Hard Accent (Gloss/Satin)  
- Applied to certain custom parts with smoother finish than suede  

### Blue Textile & Embroidery (BRIDE Pillow)  
- High-density woven fabric  
- White “BRIDE” logo embroidery

## AI GUIDELINES

### Consistency
- Keep the same **Vivid Blue** shade across ALL suede and accent parts.  
- Ensure suede texture is matte and fibrous — never glossy.

### Common AI Errors to Avoid
- Mixing suede texture with glossy plastic  
- Altering the BRIDE logo font or positioning  
- Incorrect proportions of the spherical shift knob  
- Over-blurring dashboard details  
- Inventing unrealistic carbon fiber where it doesn’t exist

### Lighting / DOF
- Use neutral interior cabin lighting.  
- Medium DOF to keep dash and console elements sharp.  
- Maintain tactile contrast between suede and plastic.

## NOTES FOR GENERATION
