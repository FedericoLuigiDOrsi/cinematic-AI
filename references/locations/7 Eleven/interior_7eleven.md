# Technical Sheet — 7-Eleven Interior (Aisle, Fridges, Checkout)

## SUBJECT OVERVIEW
Interior layout of a minimalist European-style convenience store.  
Characteristics include cool fluorescent lighting, metal shelving, beverage fridges, and a compact checkout counter.  
This location defines the film’s cold, melancholic opening mood.

## MATERIALS
- **Flooring:** Linoleum or vinyl tiles  
- **Shelving:** Metal gondola shelves with plastic price holders  
- **Fridges:** Glass doors with internal fluorescent fridge lighting  
- **Lighting:** Overhead fluorescent tubes (cool green/blue cast)  
- **Trim:** Silver metal / concrete  
- **Ceiling:** Beige linoleum or acoustic panels  

---

## GEOMETRY / FORM (FLOOR PLAN PROJECTION)
- Two parallel aisles  
- Fridge wall at rear  
- Checkout counter near entrance  
- Narrow walking space, commercial layout  
- Symmetrical shelving structure

## DETAIL CALLOUTS

### A — Aisle / Corridor
- Tall shelves  
- Cold fluorescent reflections  
- High-contrast shadows at lower shelf levels  

### B — Fridges
- Glass reflections  
- Dense product stacking  
- Cyan-tinted lighting inside  

### C — Checkout Counter
- Small workspace  
- Display racks  
- Visible cashier interaction zone  

---

## AI GUIDELINES

### Visual Consistency
- Lighting must remain **cold, fluorescent, slightly melancholic**  
- Color cast must lean toward **green/blue**, never warm  
- Maintain high-contrast between shadows and bright lights  

### Common AI Errors to Avoid
- Making the scene too warm or cozy  
- Overusing haze or fog unless intentional  
- Inventing incorrect shelf geometry  
- Exaggerating glossy reflections  

### Recommended DOF, Lighting, Reflectivity
- Moderate DOF  
- Top-down fluorescent lighting  
- Crisp highlights on wet or glossy items  
- Deep shadows beneath shelves
