## AI GUIDELINES & NOTES

### Visual Consistency
- Keep GT86 modifications accurate (louvers, spoiler).  
- Maintain the balance between **warm sunset light** and **cool artificial canopy light**.  
- Sunset must look dramatic and moody, never bright or washed-out.  
- Artificial lights should not overpower the natural sunset.

### Common AI Errors to Avoid
- Over-saturating the sunset  
- Making the lighting resemble daytime  
- Removing louvers or altering spoiler geometry  
- Incorrect reflections on concrete or car body  

### Recommended DOF, Lighting, Reflectivity
- Medium depth of field  
- Mix of warm natural sunset and cool canopy lighting  
- Concrete should be lightly reflective, not glossy  
- Car paint should reflect both warm and cool tones
